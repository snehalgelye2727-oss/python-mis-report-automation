# Python-Based Sales Report Automation

## Project Overview
This project automates restaurant-wise sales reporting using Python, Pandas, and OpenPyXL. It processes sales data and generates a structured Excel report to help monitor outlet performance and sales trends.

## Business Analysis
- **Outlet Performance:** Ranks restaurants based on Net Sales Total.
- **Top & Bottom 10 Outlets:** Identifies the highest and lowest performing outlets based on net sales.
- **Sales Channel Analysis:** Compares offline and online sales performance.
- **Platform Comparison:** Analyzes Zomato and Swiggy sales, order counts, and average bill values.
- **Order & Revenue Metrics:** Summarizes gross sales, net sales, discounts, order counts, and average bill value.

## Key Features
- Restaurant-wise sales summary and ranking.
- Top 10 and Bottom 10 outlet analysis.
- Zomato vs Swiggy comparison.
- Excel charts and conditional formatting.
- Grand totals and raw data sheet.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- OpenPyXL

## Output
Generates a formatted Excel workbook containing:
1. Restaurant Pivot
2. Top 10 & Bottom 10 Outlets
3. Raw Data

## Conclusion
This automation simplifies sales performance monitoring and helps identify outlet-level trends and differences across sales channels.
